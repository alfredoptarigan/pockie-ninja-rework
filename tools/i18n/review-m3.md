# M3 translation review

Scope: the strings the M3 world and economy milestone shows, read against `tools/i18n/source.id.json`
and `tools/i18n/glossary.json`. About 7,800 keys that are not in the M2 slice:

- Scenes and exploration zones 161, 171, 26xx-28xx (names, scene tips, the world map tooltips, explore spots).
- The NPCs and monsters of those scenes, the village NPC labels, and the Valhalla and Las Noches guards (names, titles, lines, tooltips).
- Shop, forge, equipment set, toll gate, Las Noches, pub fight, pouch and Mystery Merchant UI and system messages.
- The names, tooltips and stats text of all equipment, enchantment stones, gems and quest items.
- The main chain 10025-10193 and its four mirrors (12xxx, 14xxx, 16xxx, 18xxx), the neutral field branch quests 30051-30057 and 37601-37663, the guide quests 59xxx, and the daily quests (26xxx and the graded 2xx6xxx lists).

Identical sources were compared across the five villages, then every string was checked by script for
placeholders, tag balance, numbers, leftover Indonesian, glossary names, spacing, a/an errors, lowercase currencies
and stray CJK punctuation. The main chain and the quests were read in full; equipment tooltips were read for meaning
and name consistency.

All fixes live in `overrides.json` (merge precedence). 1,849 keys changed (1,829 new overrides and 20 updates of
M2-reviewed ones). 42 of them are in the client slice (`client/src/i18n/en.json`, regenerated).

No placeholder or number problems remain. The tag-count differences against the source are repairs of
malformed source tags (`</ font>`, a missing `<`). Source placeholders written as `{@ 0: lev}`
(`lg_Mystic_Sysinfo_006`) are kept as they are because the merge compares them to the source; the client formatter
must accept the space.

Side effects: M2-reviewed values touched (all real copy errors): quest 50004-58004 and the pouch tutorial talks
(Carrier Bag to Pouch), `lg_TaskDes_12006` and the other nation mirrors of 003, 006, 008, 022 (Smelting
Mountains for a Water, Lightning, Wind or Earth quest). No client or e2e test pins a changed text.

Not done: extending `client/scripts/game-text.ts` to the M3 scenes and NPCs. That script also writes
`client/src/game/world-data.json` (task 8) and needs the M3 data import (task 1). All M3 keys above are final in
`tools/i18n/en.json` now, so the slice only needs the key sets added.
