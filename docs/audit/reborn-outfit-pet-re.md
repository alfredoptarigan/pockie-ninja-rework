# Reborn outfits and pet items: reverse-engineering ledger

What Pockie Ninja Reborn's shipped client proves about outfit stats, stars, imprints and pet items,
and what we built from it. Sources live in `data/reborn-raw/` (gitignored): `re/main.js` (one
line, offsets below), `re/2026-10-14/main.js`, `re/serverConfig.json`, `json_items.json`,
`re/socket-events.txt`, `re/rpc/_loadData.json`, `re/rpc/_pushed.jsonl`, `re/live/loadData.json`
and the two `locales_en_translation.json`. Descriptions are ours; no Reborn code is copied.

Legend: **O** observed in the artifact, **I** inferred, **U** unknown (server-only), **A** our
assumption.

## Known differences left

- The worn outfit's "+N" badge in the equipment slots still reads the plain +N (that component is
  owned elsewhere); the bag, dialogs and names show Reborn's starred +N.
- Held weapons follow Reborn (7.6-7.9): any type shows as the outfit's own art of the same number.
  Still without weapon art: outfits 43 and 59 (no 2011 body actions or weapon SWFs), every
  outfit without an exported body or `layers.json` (Reborn-only avatars, the NPC outfits 77-83),
  and NPC "people" fighters, which we draw unarmed. Reborn's weapons 21-32 are not in our item
  table, so they are not exported. Outfit 51 has gloves and sharp SWFs but we export its gloves only, so any weapon shows the
  gloves of that number; which of the two Reborn picks is unknown (U).
